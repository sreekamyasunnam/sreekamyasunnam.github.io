Master's Capstone · MIBA Data Analytics

Faster, not wiser?

AI-generated insights vs traditional BI dashboards in managerial decision-making

Pre-registered experiment (OSF) Power BI · Generative AI Mixed-effects regression Oct 2026 – Dec 2026

The question

Many organisations are adding AI-generated insights to their BI tools. They clearly make decisions faster. But do they make them better? My capstone tests whether AI summaries help managers decide more accurately, or mainly more quickly, and when people trust the AI more than they should.

The experiment

Business master's students and early-career professionals step into a manager's shoes and solve four realistic business cases. Each case is built on a synthetic company dataset with one objectively correct answer.

Product portfolioDeciding which products to prioritise
Marketing budgetAllocating a limited budget
Customer retentionSpotting customers at risk
Supplier riskAssessing supplier reliability

Each case is shown in a different format: a Power BI dashboard, an AI-generated summary, or both together. The study measures decision accuracy, speed, confidence, overconfidence, trust and mental effort, and tests whether data literacy reduces over-reliance on AI.

The foundations

  • Cognitive Fit Theory: decisions improve when the format matches the task
  • Automation bias: the tendency to follow a system even when it is wrong
  • Algorithm aversion and appreciation: when people reject or over-trust AI advice

The outcome

A Decision-Ready BI Framework that advises organisations when to use dashboards, AI summaries or both, which safeguards help people catch AI errors, and what data literacy teams need to use AI features safely.

Every company can buy the same AI. The advantage is people who know when to question it.

Status: data collection in progress until 2 December 2026, with final submission in December 2026. Supervised at the American Institute of Applied Sciences in Switzerland.
